# FEATURES RESEARCH
## Table Stakes
- Extension: One-click add product to Wafferly from Amazon product page.
- Extension: Visual feedback (success/error toast) upon adding.
- Web/Dashboard: Create, read, update, delete (CRUD) collections/wishlists.
